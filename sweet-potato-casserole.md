# Inspiration
Paula

# Ingredients
* 3 cups mashed cooked sweet potatos
* 0.75 cups white sugar (less is more)
* 0.5 cups milk
* 1 stick melted butter
* 2 eggs, beaten
* 1 tsp vanilla

Topping
* 1 cup brown sugar
* 0.5 cups flour
* 0.5 stick melted butter
* 0.5 cups milk
* 1 cup chopped pecans

# Steps
Cook sweet potatos using preferred method and mash well

Mix rest of ingredients (except topping) and placed in greased 9x13 baking dish

Cover with mixed topping

Bake at 350 for 30-40 min
