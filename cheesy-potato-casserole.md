# Ingredients
* 1 bag frozen hashbrowns
* 16oz sour cream
* 2 cans cream of something soup (cheddar cheese, celery, or mushroom)
* 1 - 1.5 (something) milk
* 1 chopped onion (optional)
* 1 cup shredded cheese
* crushed potato chips

# Steps
Mix frozen hashbrowns, sour cream, soup, milk together in a greased 9x13 baking dish

Top with shredded cheese and crushed potato chips

Bake at 350 for 45 min
